Tulip Chair

Eero Saarinen, 1957

History

Eero Saarinen worked on the Tulip Chair from 1955, and Knoll introduced it in 1957 as part of his Pedestal Collection of chairs and tables. He disliked the tangle of legs under a typical table and wanted to replace it with a single stem.

The seat is a single moulded shell of fibreglass-reinforced plastic, on a cast aluminium base finished to look like part of the same piece. Saarinen's first idea was a chair made entirely of plastic, but the plastics of the day were not strong enough for the base.

The armchair swivels, and there are armchair and side-chair versions. Knoll still makes it.
