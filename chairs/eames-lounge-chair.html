Eames Lounge Chair

Charles and Ray Eames, 1956

History

Charles and Ray Eames designed the Lounge Chair and Ottoman for Herman Miller, and it went on sale in 1956. Charles Eames described the aim as a chair with the warm, receptive look of a well-used baseball glove.

The chair has three moulded plywood shells, one each for the back, the headrest and the seat, with a rosewood veneer on the first versions. Leather cushions sit in the shells, and the whole chair rests on a cast aluminium swivel base.

It was shown on the American television programme Home in 1956, and Herman Miller has kept it in production ever since.
