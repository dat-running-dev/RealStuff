Red and Blue Chair

Gerrit Rietveld, 1918

History

Gerrit Rietveld was a cabinetmaker in Utrecht who joined the De Stijl group of artists and architects. He designed this armchair around 1917 to 1918, and the first versions were plain, unpainted wood.

The chair is made of thirteen lengths of square-section timber and two flat boards, one for the seat and one for the back. The pieces overlap rather than being joined into a frame, so each one can be seen on its own. Rietveld later painted it: the frame black, the ends of the timber yellow, the back red and the seat blue.

The painted version, which gave the chair its name, dates from around 1923. It treated a chair as something close to abstract sculpture. The Italian firm Cassina has made licensed versions since the 1970s.
