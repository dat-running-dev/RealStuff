Panton Chair

Verner Panton, 1960

History

The Danish designer Verner Panton began sketching a chair made from one piece of material in the late 1950s. He completed the design in 1960, and it was first mass-produced in 1967 by Vitra, working with Herman Miller.

The shape is a continuous S-curve. The seat and the base are one piece, and the chair is a cantilever, held up by a single curved base with no back legs. The plastics used have changed over the years, and the chair is now made by injection moulding.

It is often described as the first cantilevered chair made from a single piece of moulded plastic.
