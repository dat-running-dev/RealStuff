Barcelona Chair

Mies van der Rohe and Lilly Reich, 1929

History

Ludwig Mies van der Rohe and Lilly Reich designed this chair for the German Pavilion at the 1929 International Exposition in Barcelona. The pavilion was a showpiece of modern architecture, and the chairs were made for its opening.

Each side of the frame is a pair of crossed steel bands, and the seat and back are padded leather panels held on straps. The proportions are generous, and the chair looks more like a piece of architecture than a piece of upholstery.

The pavilion was taken down in 1930 and rebuilt on its original site in 1986. The chair stayed in production, and Knoll has made it since the 1950s. Reich's part in the design was long credited to Mies alone, and is now more widely recognised.
