Egg Chair

Arne Jacobsen, 1958

History

Arne Jacobsen designed the Egg Chair in 1958 for the lobby and lounge areas of the SAS Royal Hotel in Copenhagen, one of the first designer hotels. He designed almost everything in the building, from the facade to the cutlery.

The chair has a moulded foam shell around a steel frame, upholstered in leather or fabric, and it tilts and swivels on a star-shaped aluminium base. The tall, curved sides give the person sitting in it some privacy in a public room.

Fritz Hansen, the Danish firm that first made the chair, still produces it.
