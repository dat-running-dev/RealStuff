Chair Guide

Nine chairs, in order.

From a country chair of the early 1700s to a plastic one of 1960. The space between chairs follows the gap between their dates, shortened so chairs made in the same decade stay readable.

  1. early 1700s

    Windsor chair

    English chairmakers, early 1700s

    A spindle back, a solid wooden seat, and legs fitted straight into it.

    1918

    Red and Blue Chair

    Gerrit Rietveld, 1918

    Thirteen lengths of timber and two boards, painted to show how they join.

    1925

    Wassily Chair

    Marcel Breuer, 1925

    Bent steel tube, inspired by bicycle handlebars, with a sling seat and back.

    Barcelona Chair

    Mies van der Rohe and Lilly Reich, 1929

    1956

    Eames Lounge Chair

    Charles and Ray Eames, 1956

  7. 1 year1 year after the previous chair

    1957

    Tulip Chair

    Eero Saarinen, 1957

    One pedestal instead of four legs, to clear the clutter under a table.

  8. 1 year1 year after the previous chair

    1958

    Egg Chair

    Arne Jacobsen, 1958

    Made for a hotel lobby, to give guests a quiet corner in a busy room.
